## 01. 城市生活与乡村生活，你喜欢哪个？

City Life or Country Life?

**题号：** #M077055 / #F101062

**英文原题：** Living in the countryside or having a city life, which one do you prefer? Please use examples or your personal experience to support your opinion.

生活在乡村还是城市，你更喜欢哪一种？请用例子或个人经历支持你的观点。

**英文正文：219词**

### 第1段

Whether city life is better than country life has been widely discussed. Although country life is quiet, I believe city life is better for me because city life saves travel time and it is easier to get medical help in cities.

关于“城市生活是否比乡村生活好”已有很多讨论。虽然乡村生活很安静，但我认为城市生活更适合我，因为城市生活节省出行时间，而且在城市更容易得到医疗帮助。

### 第2段

First, city life saves travel time. This is because shops and schools are usually nearby. People do not need to travel far for simple tasks. They can use that time for other things. This is useful for people who work or study every day and have little free time. For example, I can walk to a shop in five minutes. This helps me save time.

城市生活节省出行时间。这是因为商店和学校通常就在附近。人们办简单的事情不用走很远，可以把时间用在其他事情上。这对每天工作或学习、空闲时间很少的人很有用。例如，我走五分钟就能到商店。这帮助我节省时间。

### 第3段

Second, it is easier to get medical help in cities. This is because there are more hospitals nearby. When someone feels ill, a long journey can be difficult. A nearby hospital makes getting help easier. Families can also visit sick relatives more easily when the hospital is close to their own homes. For example, I can reach a hospital quickly when I feel ill. My family can take me there and bring me home. We do not spend hours on the road. This makes my life easier.

在城市更容易得到医疗帮助。这是因为附近有更多医院。人不舒服时，长途出行可能很困难，附近的医院让求助更容易。医院离家近时，家人也更容易探望生病的亲人。例如，我不舒服时可以很快到医院。家人可以送我去，再接我回家，不用在路上花几小时。这让我的生活更方便。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe city life is better for me because city life saves travel time and it is easier to get medical help in cities.

总之，我认为城市生活更适合我，因为城市生活节省出行时间，而且在城市更容易得到医疗帮助。

## 02. 名人是否要放弃隐私

Fame and Privacy

**题号：** #M077053 / #F101134

**英文原题：** People who are famous entertainers or sportspeople should give up the right to privacy because this is the price of fame. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this point of view? Give your reasons or use your own experiences to support.

**英文正文：217词**

### 第1段

Whether famous people should give up their privacy has been widely discussed. Although fans want to know about their lives, I believe famous people should keep their privacy because privacy helps them rest and privacy protects their family life.

关于“名人是否应该放弃隐私”已有很多讨论。虽然粉丝想了解他们的生活，但我认为名人应该保留隐私，因为隐私帮助他们休息，而且隐私保护他们的家庭生活。

### 第2段

First, privacy helps them rest. This is because they need quiet time after work. Their work may be public, but their home should still be a quiet place where they can relax. Constant attention can make a normal day tiring. Everyone needs a break from work and other people's questions. For example, a singer can rest at home without cameras outside. The singer can sleep, cook and spend time with friends. These are simple things that everyone needs. This helps the singer feel less stressed.

隐私帮助他们休息。这是因为他们工作后需要安静的时间。他们的工作可能面向公众，但家仍应该是可以安静放松的地方。持续受到关注会让普通的一天也很累，每个人都需要暂时离开工作和别人的追问。例如，歌手可以在家休息，外面没有相机拍摄。歌手可以睡觉、做饭、和朋友相处，这些是每个人都需要的普通事情。这帮助歌手减轻压力。

### 第3段

Second, privacy protects their family life. This is because their children also need a normal life. Family members did not choose a public job. They should not lose their freedom because someone else is famous. For example, an actor can take a child to school without being followed. The child can enter school quietly and meet friends. A normal school day should not become a public event. This makes family life easier.

隐私保护他们的家庭生活。这是因为他们的孩子也需要正常生活。家人没有选择面向公众的工作，不应该因为亲人成名而失去自由。例如，演员可以不被跟拍地送孩子上学。孩子可以安静地进学校、见朋友，普通的上学日不应该变成公开活动。这让家庭生活更轻松。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe famous people should keep their privacy because privacy helps them rest and privacy protects their family life.

总之，我认为名人应该保留隐私，因为隐私帮助他们休息，而且隐私保护他们的家庭生活。

关于“年轻人是否应等到25岁才能开车”已有很多讨论。虽然开车可能有危险，但我认为18岁是开始开车的合适年龄，因为年轻人需要时间学习道路安全，而且18岁可以开车让日常生活更方便。

### 第2段

Before they drive alone, they should learn how to stop, turn and share the road with other people. For example, I took driving lessons at 18 and learned to check my mirrors. My teacher asked me to look carefully before moving. I practised this many times until it became a habit. This helped me drive more safely.

年轻人需要时间学习道路安全。这是因为他们开车前必须理解交通规则。独自开车前，他们应学会停车、转弯，以及和其他人共同使用道路。例如，我18岁时上驾驶课，学会查看后视镜。老师要求我开动车前认真观察，我练了很多次，直到形成习惯。这帮助我更安全地开车。

### 第3段

Second, driving at 18 makes daily life easier. This is because young adults need to travel to school or work. Waiting until 25 could make some young adults depend on their parents for several more years. For example, I drove to college instead of waiting for a bus. I could leave home at a suitable time and arrive before class. My parents did not need to take me. This helped me save time.

18岁可以开车让日常生活更方便。这是因为年轻成年人需要去上学或上班。等到25岁可能让部分年轻人多依赖父母好几年。例如，我开车去大学，不用等公交。我能在合适的时间出门，上课前到达，父母不用送我。这帮助我节省时间。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe 18 is a suitable age to start driving because young people need time to learn road safety and driving at 18 makes daily life easier.

总之，我认为18岁是开始开车的合适年龄，因为年轻人需要时间学习道路安全，而且18岁可以开车让日常生活更方便。

## 04. 国家把钱花在建筑修复，而非现代住房

Protecting Old Buildings and Building New Homes

**题号：** #M077020 / #F101138

**英文原题：** Many countries spend large amounts of money on the restoration of historic buildings rather than on modern housing. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this analysis? What are advantages and disadvantages of this? Support your writing with your experience or examples.

许多国家花大量资金修复历史建筑，而不是建设现代住房。你在多大程度上同意或不同意这种做法？它有哪些优点和缺点？请结合自己的经历或例子说明。

**英文正文：218词**

**问法提醒：** 第二段是保护古建筑的好处，第三段是资金取舍的坏处；问到住房时，用第三段回应住房。

### 第1段

Whether governments should spend money on old buildings has been widely discussed. Although new homes are also needed, I believe old buildings should be protected, but new homes also need money because old buildings help people learn history and building repairs can leave less money for new homes.

关于“政府是否应该花钱保护古建筑”已有很多讨论。虽然人们也需要新住房，但我认为古建筑应该保护，但新住房也需要资金，因为古建筑帮助人们学习历史，而且修复建筑可能挤占新住房的资金。

### 第2段

First, old buildings help people learn history. This is because people can see how others lived in the past. Schools can take students to these places. A short visit can make a history lesson more interesting and easier to remember. For example, I visited an old house and saw an old kitchen. This helped me understand life in the past.

古建筑帮助人们学习历史。这是因为人们能看到过去的人怎样生活。学校可以带学生参观，短暂参观能让历史课更有趣、更容易记住。例如，我参观古老住宅，看到了旧式厨房。这帮助我理解过去的生活。

### 第3段

Second, building repairs can leave less money for new homes. This is because governments cannot spend the same money twice. Some families need a safe place to live now. A beautiful old building cannot meet that basic need. Repair costs can also continue for years. Money is needed again when roofs, walls or windows become damaged. For example, an expensive repair project may leave no money for new flats. This can make it harder to provide enough homes.

修复建筑可能挤占新住房的资金。这是因为政府不能把同一笔钱花两次。有些家庭现在就需要安全的住所，一座美丽的古建筑无法满足这一基本需要。修复费用可能持续多年，屋顶、墙壁或窗户损坏时还需要再花钱。例如，昂贵的修复工程可能使政府没钱建新公寓。这可能使政府更难提供足够住房。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe old buildings should be protected, but new homes also need money because old buildings help people learn history and building repairs can leave less money for new homes.

总之，我认为古建筑应该保护，但新住房也需要资金，因为古建筑帮助人们学习历史，而且修复建筑可能挤占新住房的资金。

## 05. 法律改变人的行为

Can Laws Change People's Behaviour?

**题号：** #M077018 / #F101003

**英文原题：** Some people think human behaviors can be changed by laws, while others think laws have little effect. What is your opinion.

有人认为法律可以改变人的行为，另一些人认为法律作用很小。你的观点是什么？

**英文正文：214词**

### 第1段

Whether laws can change people's behaviour has been widely discussed. Although some people still break laws, I believe laws can change behaviour because punishment can stop people from breaking rules and laws tell people what they must do.

关于“法律是否可以改变人的行为”已有很多讨论。虽然有些人仍然违法，但我认为法律可以改变行为，因为惩罚可以阻止人们违反规则，而且法律告诉人们必须做什么。

### 第2段

First, punishment can stop people from breaking rules. This is because people do not want to pay fines. The rule should be clear and used fairly. People are more likely to follow it when they understand the result. For example, drivers may slow down when speeding leads to a fine. A driver may check the speed more often after receiving a fine. The same mistake is then less likely to happen again. This can make roads safer.

惩罚可以阻止人们违反规则。这是因为人们不想交罚款。规则应清楚并公平执行，人们理解后果时更可能遵守。例如，超速会被罚款时，司机可能减速。司机被罚款后可能更常检查车速，同样的错误就较少再次发生。这可以让道路更安全。

### 第3段

Second, laws tell people what they must do. This is because clear rules make the right action easier to understand. Clear rules can also make daily decisions easier. People do not have to guess what other people will do. For example, drivers stop at red lights because the rule is clear. Other drivers and people on foot can then cross safely. Everyone knows when to move and when to wait. This helps people behave properly.

法律告诉人们必须做什么。这是因为清楚的规则使人更容易知道该怎么做。清楚的规则也让日常决定更容易，人们不用猜别人会怎么做。例如，司机在红灯时停车，因为规则很清楚。其他司机和行人就能安全通行，大家知道何时走、何时等。这帮助人们正确行动。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe laws can change behaviour because punishment can stop people from breaking rules and laws tell people what they must do.

总之，我认为法律可以改变行为，因为惩罚可以阻止人们违反规则，而且法律告诉人们必须做什么。

## 06. 政府应该改善公共交通还是多修路

**英文原题：** As cities are expanding, some people claim governments should look forward to creating better networks of public transport available for everyone rather than building more roads for vehicle owning population. What’s your opinion? Give some examples or experience to support.

随着城市扩大，有人认为政府应优先改善人人可用的公共交通网络，而不是为汽车使用者修更多道路。你的观点是什么？请用例子或经历支持观点。

**英文正文：210词**

### 第1段

Whether governments should improve public transport rather than build more roads has been widely discussed. Although more roads can help drivers, I believe public transport should come first because public transport saves people money and public transport can reduce traffic.

关于“政府是否应该优先改善公共交通而不是多修路”已有很多讨论。虽然更多道路能帮助司机，但我认为应该优先改善公共交通，因为公共交通帮助人们省钱，而且公共交通可以减少拥堵。

### 第2段

First, public transport saves people money. This is because bus fares can cost less than driving and parking. People who do not own a car can use the same service. Better transport can help them reach more places. For example, I spend three dollars on a bus instead of ten dollars on parking. I can use the money I save to buy food or pay other bills. This matters to my daily budget. This helps me save money.

公共交通帮助人们省钱。这是因为公交票价可能比开车和停车的费用低。没有车的人也能使用同样的服务，更好的交通能帮助他们去更多地方。例如，我花三元坐公交，不用花十元停车。我能把省下的钱用来买食物或付账单，这对日常预算很重要。这帮助我省钱。

### 第3段

Second, public transport can reduce traffic. This is because one bus can carry many people. A useful bus service should be frequent and easy to reach. People need it to fit their daily plans. For example, a full bus can replace many cars. If more workers take that bus, there will be fewer cars near the office during the busiest part of the day. This can make the roads less crowded.

公共交通可以减少拥堵。这是因为一辆公交车能载很多人。好用的公交应班次多、容易到达，人们需要它配合每天的安排。例如，一辆满载的公交可以代替很多汽车。如果更多员工坐那辆公交，一天最忙时办公室附近的汽车就会减少。这可以让道路不那么拥挤。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe public transport should come first because public transport saves people money and public transport can reduce traffic.

总之，我认为应该优先改善公共交通，因为公共交通帮助人们省钱，而且公共交通可以减少拥堵。

The child can then choose a safer place to play. This helps prevent damage.

承担责任让家长更留意。这是因为家长发现问题时可能更早采取行动。必要时家长也应向学校求助，成年人传达一致的信息时，孩子更容易学会。例如，家长可以阻止孩子向窗户扔石头。家长可以解释碎玻璃可能伤人，孩子就能换个更安全的地方玩。这帮助防止损坏。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe parents should share responsibility when their young children cause harm because parents can teach children basic rules and responsibility makes parents pay more attention.

总之，我认为年幼孩子造成伤害时，家长应分担责任，因为家长可以教孩子基本规则，而且承担责任让家长更留意。

是否应该给高收入人群设置最高工资？你在多大程度上同意？请给出理由或论据。

**题号：** #M071012 / #F101123

**英文原题：** With the increase of digital media available online, the role of the library has become obsolete. Universities should only procure new digital media rather than constantly update textbooks. Discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this position and give your own point of view.

随着网上数字资料增多，有人认为图书馆的作用已经过时，大学应只购买新的数字资料，而不是持续更新纸质教材。请讨论这个主张的优缺点，并给出自己的观点。

**英文正文：212词**

**问法提醒：** 第二段是电子书的好处，第三段是电子书的局限。结论保留纸质书，不能说完全替代。

### 第1段

Whether universities should replace paper books with digital books has been widely discussed. Although digital books are useful, I believe universities should use digital books but keep some paper books because digital books can save printing costs and digital books are hard to use without a working device.

关于“大学是否应该用电子书替代纸质书”已有很多讨论。虽然电子书有用，但我认为大学应该用电子书，但也保留一些纸质书，因为电子书可以节省印刷费用，而且没有能用的设备时，电子书很难使用。

### 第2段

First, digital books can save printing costs. This is because the same file can be used without printing more pages. Universities often need many copies of the same material. Sharing a file can reduce the need to print and carry large piles of paper. For example, I read class notes on a screen instead of printing them. This helps save paper and money.

电子书可以节省印刷费用。这是因为同一个文件可以重复使用，不必多印纸张。大学常需要很多份相同资料，共享文件能减少印刷和搬运大量纸张的需要。例如，我在屏幕上读课堂笔记，不把它们打印出来。这帮助节省纸张和金钱。

### 第3段

Second, digital books are hard to use without a working device. This is because students need a phone or computer with enough power. For example, I cannot read an electronic book when my phone battery is dead. If the library still has a paper copy, I can continue studying. Without it, I may have to wait until I can charge my phone. Keeping paper books gives students another choice.

没有能用的设备时，电子书很难使用。这是因为学生需要有电的手机或电脑。例如，手机没电时，我看不了电子书。如果图书馆有纸质本，我能继续学；没有的话，可能要等手机充电。保留纸质书让学生多一个选择。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe universities should use digital books but keep some paper books because digital books can save printing costs and digital books are hard to use without a working device.

总之，我认为大学应该用电子书，但也保留一些纸质书，因为电子书可以节省印刷费用，而且没有能用的设备时，电子书很难使用。

## 34. 与书本，正统教育相比，人生经历经验是更好的老师？

Life Experience or Books?

**题号：** #M071011 / #F101122

**英文原题：** Some people argue that experience is the best teacher. Life experiences can teach more effectively than books or formal school education. How far do you agree with this idea? Support your opinion with reasons and/or your personal experience.

有人认为经验是最好的老师，生活经历比书本或正规学校教育更有效。你在多大程度上同意？请用理由或个人经历支持观点。

**英文正文：218词**

### 第1段

Whether life experience teaches better than books has been widely discussed. Although books provide useful information, I believe experience is often a better teacher because personal experience is easier to remember and experience helps people solve real problems.

关于“生活经验是否比书本教得更好”已有很多讨论。虽然书本提供有用信息，但我认为经验通常是更好的老师，因为亲身经历更容易记住，而且经验帮助人们解决实际问题。

### 第2段

First, personal experience is easier to remember. This is because people remember what happens to them. Advice in a book may seem unimportant until a similar problem happens. Experience can show why that advice matters in real life. For example, I remember missing a bus because I left home late. I had to wait for the next bus and arrived late. After that day, I checked the time before leaving home. This taught me to leave earlier.

亲身经历更容易记住。这是因为人们会记得发生在自己身上的事情。书里的建议在遇到类似问题前可能显得不重要，经验能让人理解它的实际作用。例如，我记得自己因为出门晚而错过公交。我不得不等下一班车，最后迟到了，从那以后出门前会看好时间。这教会我早点出门。

### 第3段

Second, experience helps people solve real problems. This is because people learn by trying a task themselves. Books can still explain the basic steps. The best learning often comes when people use that information to do something themselves. For example, I learned to cook by making a simple meal. I noticed when the food needed more time and checked it again. Those small decisions became easier the next time I cooked. This helped me do the task by myself.

经验帮助人们解决实际问题。这是因为人们通过亲自尝试来学习。书本仍能解释基本步骤，但人们亲自用这些知识做事时，往往学得更好。例如，我通过做一道简单的菜学会做饭。我发现食物还需要时间，就再检查一次，下次做饭时这些小决定就容易了。这帮助我自己完成这件事。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe experience is often a better teacher because personal experience is easier to remember and experience helps people solve real problems.

总之，我认为经验通常是更好的老师，因为亲身经历更容易记住，而且经验帮助人们解决实际问题。

## 35. 高中学习百年前戏剧的利弊

Studying Old Plays at School

**题号：** #M071007 / #F101028

**英文原题：** There are both problems and benefits for high school students to study plays and works of theaters written centuries ago. Discuss and use your own experience.

高中生学习几百年前写成的戏剧作品既有问题也有好处。请讨论，并结合自己的经历说明。

**英文正文：217词**

**问法提醒：** 第二段写好处，第三段写困难和简单办法。

### 第1段

Whether students should study plays written long ago has been widely discussed. Although old plays can be difficult, I believe old plays can be useful, but students need help because old plays help students learn about the past and old language can be hard to understand.

关于“学生是否应该学习很久以前写的戏剧”已有很多讨论。虽然古老戏剧可能很难，但我认为古老戏剧可能有用，但学生需要帮助，因为古老戏剧帮助学生了解过去，而且古老的语言可能很难理解。

### 第2段

First, old plays help students learn about the past. This is because stories show old ways of living. For example, I read a play that showed how families lived long ago. The family followed rules that were different from mine. Talking about those differences made the history lesson more interesting to me. This helped me understand history.

古老戏剧帮助学生了解过去。这是因为故事会展示过去的生活方式。例如，我读过一部展示过去家庭生活的戏剧。故事中的家庭遵循和我家不同的规则，讨论这些差别让我更感兴趣。这帮助我理解历史。

### 第3段

Second, old language can be hard to understand. This is because some words are not used in daily life today. Help should be simple and close to the text. Short notes and a clear classroom discussion can remove much of the difficulty. For example, I needed my teacher to explain several old words. After the explanation, I could follow the story without stopping so often. The play became more enjoyable once the language was clearer. Simple notes can make the play easier to read.

古老的语言可能很难理解。这是因为有些词现在日常生活中不用了。帮助应简单、贴近文本，简短注释和清楚的课堂讨论能消除很多困难。例如，我需要老师解释几个古老的词。解释后，我不用经常停下就能跟上故事，语言清楚后，戏剧也更有趣。简单的注释可以让戏剧更容易读懂。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe old plays can be useful, but students need help because old plays help students learn about the past and old language can be hard to understand.

总之，我认为古老戏剧可能有用，但学生需要帮助，因为古老戏剧帮助学生了解过去，而且古老的语言可能很难理解。

## 36. 大众传媒影响社会与个性

How Media Shape Opinions and Behaviour

**题号：** #F101010

**英文原题：** The mass media, including TV, radio and newspapers, influence our society and shape our opinions and characters. What is your opinion?

电视、广播和报纸等大众传媒影响社会，并塑造人们的观点和性格。你的观点是什么？

**英文正文：224词**

### 第1段

Whether media shape people's views and behaviour has been widely discussed. Although people can think for themselves, I believe media influence both views and behaviour because news changes how people see problems and programmes can encourage new habits.

关于“传媒是否塑造人们的观点和行为”已有很多讨论。虽然人们可以独立思考，但我认为传媒既影响观点也影响行为，因为新闻改变人们看待问题的方式，而且节目可以鼓励新的习惯。

### 第2段

First, news changes how people see problems. This is because it gives people information they did not have before. People cannot visit every place or see every problem themselves. News provides part of the information they use to form opinions. The stories that receive the most attention may seem the most important. This can affect what people discuss with friends and family. For example, I cared more about litter after watching a report about dirty beaches. This changed my view of the problem.

新闻改变人们看待问题的方式。这是因为新闻提供人们以前不知道的信息。人们不能亲自去每个地方、看每个问题，新闻提供了形成观点的部分信息。得到最多关注的新闻可能显得最重要，影响人们和亲友讨论什么。例如，看过海滩垃圾的报道后，我更在意乱扔垃圾的问题。这改变了我对这个问题的看法。

### 第3段

Second, programmes can encourage new habits. This is because people may copy useful actions they see. The effect is not always immediate. Repeated messages can slowly make a different action feel normal in daily life. For example, I started using a shopping bag again after watching a programme about waste. I kept the bag near the door so I would remember it. Soon, I took it with me every time I went shopping. This helped me change a daily habit.

节目可以鼓励新的习惯。这是因为人们可能模仿看到的有用行为。影响不总立刻出现，反复的信息能慢慢让另一种行为成为日常习惯。例如，看过关于垃圾的节目后，我开始重复使用购物袋。我把袋子放在门边提醒自己，很快每次购物都会带上它。这帮助我改变一个日常习惯。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe media influence both views and behaviour because news changes how people see problems and programmes can encourage new habits.

总之，我认为传媒既影响观点也影响行为，因为新闻改变人们看待问题的方式，而且节目可以鼓励新的习惯。

## 37. 外语应该成为必修课吗

Should Foreign Languages Be Compulsory?

**题号：** #F101137

**英文原题：** Some people think learning a foreign language at school should be compulsory. To what extent do you agree with it? Use your experience or examples to support your viewpoint.

有人认为学校应把外语设为必修课。你在多大程度上同意？请结合自己的经历或例子支持观点。

**英文正文：221词**

### 第1段

Whether all students should learn a foreign language has been widely discussed. Although learning a language takes time, I believe schools should require students to learn a foreign language because language skills make travel easier and language skills can help at work.

关于“是否所有学生都应该学习外语”已有很多讨论。虽然学习一门语言需要时间，但我认为学校应该要求学生学习外语，因为外语能力让旅行更方便，而且外语能力可以在工作中帮忙。

### 第2段

First, language skills make travel easier. This is because travellers can ask for help directly. Travel often involves small questions about food, tickets and directions. A little language knowledge can make these moments much easier. Starting at school gives every student a chance to learn. They do not need to wait until a problem happens abroad. For example, I use English to ask where the bus stop is. This helps me find my way.

外语能力让旅行更方便。这是因为旅行者可以直接求助。旅行常要询问食物、票和方向，懂一点外语就能让这些小事容易很多。在学校开始学，让每个学生都有机会，不必等在国外遇到问题才学。例如，我用英语询问公交站在哪里。这帮助我找到路。

### 第3段

Second, language skills can help at work. This is because workers may need to speak to foreign customers. Even a simple job may involve people from other countries. Clear communication can prevent mistakes and help customers feel welcome. For example, a shop worker uses English to help a visitor buy a ticket. The worker can explain the price and the time clearly. The visitor can then choose the right ticket without confusion. This helps the worker serve more customers.

外语能力可以在工作中帮忙。这是因为员工可能需要与外国顾客交流。即使简单工作也可能接触外国人，清楚交流能避免错误，让顾客感到受欢迎。例如，店员用英语帮助游客买票。员工能清楚解释价格和时间，游客就能不迷糊地选对票。这帮助员工接待更多顾客。

### 第4段

In conclusion, I believe schools should require students to learn a foreign language because language skills make travel easier and language skills can help at work.

总之，我认为学校应该要求学生学习外语，因为外语能力让旅行更方便，而且外语能力可以在工作中帮忙。
